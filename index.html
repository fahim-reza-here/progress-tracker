        📖 Complete User Manual

        🎯 Getting Started

        Enter your Name & Batch in the welcome screen. Sign in with Google to sync across devices, or continue as Guest. Your profile is editable anytime via the ✏️ button.

        ✅ Tracking Chapters

        Click any chapter 1st time = Half Done (+50 XP). Click 2nd time = Fully Done (+100 XP). Click 3rd time = Undo. The date sticker shows when you completed it.

        🔄 Revision System

        Only fully completed chapters can be revised. Max 2 revisions/day per chapter (+20 XP each). Revision XP adds to your total chapter XP display. Use this to boost your rank!

        🔥 Streak System

        Maintain daily activity to keep your streak alive. Streak Freeze: You get a 1-day gap allowance! Study today, skip tomorrow, study day after → streak continues. 2+ day gap breaks streak.

        ⭐ Level Up System

        Earn 500 XP to level up. Every level requires 500 XP. Your level badge shows current level. The XP bar shows progress toward next level.

        🌳 Aura Tree Evolution

        Your tree evolves as you progress: 🌱 Sprout (0-25%) → 🌿 Growth (26-50%) → 🌸 Sakura (51-75%) → 🌳✨ Cosmic (76-100%). Complete chapters to watch your tree grow!

        🏆 Achievement Badges

        Unlock 7 badges: First Milestone, Syllabus Conqueror, Daily Warrior, Weekly Champion, Monthly Legend, Streak Master, Elite Level 5. Hover over badges to see requirements.

        ⚡ Subject Mastery Badges

        Complete ALL chapters of a subject (Full ✅) to unlock animated glowing badges! Each subject has a unique badge. Check your progress in the Subject Mastery section.

        🌍 Global Leaderboard

        Compete with all students. Filter by Batch (HSC 25/26/27/28/Others) and Time Period (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Yearly, Lifetime). Click names to see details (Rank Pushers only).

        📈 Personal Leaderboard (Rank Pushers)

        Exclusive leaderboard for Rank Pushers group members. Admin adds members to this special group. Only group members can see this tab and each other's detailed stats.

        🔒 Rank Pushers Group

        Special members-only group managed by admin. Benefits: access to Personal Leaderboard, Fresh Start feature, Exam Date feature, and detailed user stats. Contact admin to join.

        🔄 Fresh Start

        Archive your current progress and start a new round! Requirements: 1) Must be signed in, 2) Must be in Rank Pushers group, 3) Must have 100% syllabus completion. Keeps all previous XP!

        🎯 Exam Date Countdown

        Set your exam date to see countdown. Rank Pushers only! Shows days remaining, exam today alert, or passed status. Visible to others when they click your name in leaderboard.

        📅 Study Calendar

        Monthly calendar view with color-coded activity levels. Navigate months with arrows. Click any date to see detailed activity log for that day (chapters, revisions, XP earned).

        📊 Progress Analytics

        Bar chart showing completion percentage per subject. Updates in real-time as you mark chapters. Helps identify weak subjects that need more attention.

        📚 My Study History

        Click the Chapters Done card to see complete history. All activities sorted by date: Full chapters, Half chapters, Revisions. Shows round number for archived activities.

        ✨ XP Breakdown

        Click the Total XP card to see detailed breakdown: Chapter Completions + Revisions + Archived Rounds. Shows how your total XP is calculated.

        🤖 Buddy Quotes

        Motivational quotes & Islamic ayats auto-rotate every 7 seconds. Use arrows to browse manually. Click ✏️ to rename your buddy or add custom quotes. Your personal motivation engine!

        🔗 Related Links

        Save personal study materials, YouTube playlists, and resources. Pre-loaded default links included. Add, edit, delete your own links. Links open in new tab.

        ⚙️ Custom Syllabus

        Add your own subjects and chapters! Select subject, paper, type chapter name, and click Add. Custom chapters persist across resets. Perfect for coaching-specific topics.

        🧹 Reset Options

        Two reset options: 1) Reset Current Round Only — clears current progress, keeps previous XP and archived rounds. 2) Reset All & Back to Round 1 — full reset, keeps custom subjects + preloaded subjects.

        🌓 Dark/Light Mode

        Toggle between dark and light themes with the 🌓 button. Your preference is saved. All UI elements adapt including charts, cards, and text.

        🔊 Sound Effects

        Toggle sound on/off with the 🔊 button. Plays cheerful sounds when completing chapters and revision sounds when revising. Muted by default for focus.

        🔐 Sign In / Sync

        Sign in with Google or Email to sync across devices. All data (chapters, XP, syllabus, links) syncs to Firebase. Guest users can use locally but data stays on one device.

        🛡️ Privacy & Security

        Data stored securely via Firebase. Name, batch, and progress are visible to others. Email is private. Read the Privacy Policy for full details. Admin can block accounts if needed.
